About Furnitur

We design and curate furniture that blends timeless craftsmanship with modern living — pieces made to be lived in, not just looked at.

Our Story

Furnitur started with a simple idea: furniture should feel as good as it looks. What began as a small workshop sourcing oak and stainless steel has grown into a curated catalog of chairs, sofas, lighting, and decor — each piece chosen for its honest materials and clean, functional design.

We work directly with a small network of makers who share our obsession with quality joinery, sustainable sourcing, and finishes that age gracefully. No mass-produced filler — every product in our shop earns its place.
